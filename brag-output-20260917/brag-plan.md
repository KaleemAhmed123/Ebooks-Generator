# Brag Plan: TypeScript to Deployment

## 1. What is the app?
A highly custom, zero-bloat Markdown-to-PDF eBook factory generating the comprehensive volume "TypeScript to Deployment: The Ultimate Guide", encompassing 9 individual booklets.

## 2. What is the funniest or most impressive claim?
"KNOW WHAT YOU SHIP. OWN WHAT YOU RUN." and the sheer volume: 9 booklets, each with its own dedicated focus and cover design.

## 3. What is the visual hook?
The dynamic flashing of the 9 original covers. Each cover has its own accent color, terminal block, and stack text. We will cycle through them at a rapid pace to show the scale of the content.

## 4. What should be shown from the actual UI?
The 9 original cover designs, accurately rendered using their `meta.json` configurations (title, accent color, banner, kicker), snapping to the beat of the music.

## 5. What is the shortest satisfying video?
35 seconds.

## 6. What tone fits best?
Chaotic / Fast-Paced. A high-energy, beat-synced showcase that sells the massive amount of content across the 9 booklets.

## 7. What should the audio feel like?
Dense rhythmic layer or fast chaotic music. The audio arc starts punchy, hits a frenetic pace during the 9-cover flash, and slams into a heavy cinematic beat for the final master volume.

## 8. What should the share caption say?
"TypeScript to Deployment: The Ultimate Guide. 9 booklets. 1 volume. Know what you ship, own what you run."

## 9. What's the user flow worth showing?
The progression from individual, highly focused topics (TypeScript, Next.js, Node, Data, AWS, etc.) slamming together into the master volume.

## Storyboard (35s)
- **0s - 4s (Hook):** Deep space navy background. A blinking terminal cursor types `$ tsc --noEmit && npm test`. Sudden green flash: `✓ 0 errors`.
- **4s - 8s (The Premise):** Text slam: "YOU DON'T NEED A COURSE." "YOU NEED A REFERENCE."
- **8s - 22s (The 9 Covers Flash):** A rapid, beat-synced sequence showing the original covers of the 9 booklets. 
  - 01-TypeScript (Accent `#2b5fa8`)
  - 02-Next.js
  - 03-Node Core
  - 04-Ecosystem
  - 05-Data
  - 06-API Design
  - 07-AI SDKs
  - 08-Deployment
  - 09-AI Practices
  *(Each flashes for ~1.5 seconds, snapping to a strong beat, showcasing the unique accent color and banner).*
- **22s - 27s (The Master Build):** Fast cuts of the 9 booklets stacking together into one massive volume.
- **27s - 32s (Tagline Slam):** Big bold text slams in: "KNOW WHAT YOU SHIP. OWN WHAT YOU RUN."
- **32s - 35s (Outro):** The master cover accent color (`#1f6f8b`) fades in: "The Ultimate Guide. Available now."
