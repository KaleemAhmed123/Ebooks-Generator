## SVD — the fundamental matrix decomposition

- **SVD** (Singular Value Decomposition) — every matrix A of any shape factors as `A = UΣVᵀ`. U and V are orthogonal (rotation) matrices; Σ is diagonal (scaling). Three matrices, each doing one geometric job
- **Singular values** σ₁ ≥ σ₂ ≥ … ≥ σᵣ — the scaling factors along each axis. Large σ means the matrix strongly amplifies that direction; zero σ means that direction is collapsed entirely. The number of non-zero singular values = rank(A)
- **Truncated SVD** — keeping only the top-k singular values and their vectors gives the best rank-k approximation of A (the Eckart-Young theorem). This is the foundation of compression, denoising, and low-rank adapters
