# AI Engineering: From Scratch

## Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

RLHF works beautifully, but it is a massive engineering headache. It requires holding three different massive models in VRAM simultaneously: the Actor, the Critic, and the Reward Model. It is notoriously unstable.

### The DPO Breakthrough

In 2023, DPO mathematicaly proved that you don't need a separate Reward Model or PPO at all.

You can take the human preference data (Answer A is better than Answer B) and use it to update the LLM directly via a simple classification loss function. 
- You increase the probability of the tokens in the winning answer.
- You decrease the probability of the tokens in the losing answer.

### Why DPO Won

DPO is stable, requires fewer hyperparameters, and demands significantly less compute. It quickly became the default alignment algorithm for the open-source community, powering the alignment of Llama 3, Mistral, and Qwen. RLHF remains slightly more capable at the absolute bleeding edge, but DPO democratized alignment.
