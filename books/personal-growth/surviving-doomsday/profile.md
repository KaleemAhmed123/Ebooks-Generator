## Strategist's read

1. **The bottleneck is distribution, not skill.** The resume shows unusually strong production depth for 2.5 years: idempotency, ledgers, authorization models, eval gates, measured business outcomes. None of it is being turned into money, because no buyer sees it. Research on brand and clients matters more here than research on judgement or taste.
2. **The $5k/month target is about 6× current pay** (if ₹70k/month). A promotion in the Indian salaried market is unlikely to get there in 12 months. Plausible paths are foreign clients, a remote role paid in USD, or a mix. Tracks 01 and 07 should check real rates; don't assume.
3. **"Senior" in 12 months at about 3 years of experience** is a title fight likely lost on big-company ladders. Freelance clients pay for outcomes, not titles, and the case studies already read as senior.
4. **The attention numbers are the biggest controllable lever.** Moving 10 of the 20 hours of YouTube and Instagram into shipping and publishing doubles effective capacity at zero cost. Without that change, any plan that needs 15–20 hours a week is fiction.
5. **Hiding the work is the costliest habit.** Publishable material already exists: case studies, ebooks, blogs, and production stories with numbers. Brand building here means distributing what exists, not creating from scratch.
6. **There are unusual niche edges to test.** Arabic study plus RTL production experience plus a humanitarian product could point to MENA/Gulf Arabic-first work. Primble is insurance-document AI with a measured result. The SAP/Salesforce reconciliation work is enterprise integration. These are hypotheses for track 05 to test, not conclusions.
7. **The runway is fine, but sales is the untested risk.** Six months of runway covers a freelance ramp only if the first deal closes early. Zero closed deals means the first deliverable is one paid engagement, of any size.

8. **The sales refusal clashes with the goals.** The 3-year goal includes "wants to be able to sell", there are zero closed deals, and manual sales work is refused. Automation can handle top-of-funnel work: lead lists, content distribution, follow-up sequences. It can't close a first deal for someone with no network or reputation, because early deals close on conversations and trust. The research tracks should find the *least manual* path, such as inbound from published work, platforms, or referrals through agency colleagues. Don't pretend the path has no human sales step at all.
