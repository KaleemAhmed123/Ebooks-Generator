## Platforms and AI demand

[S1] Who Is AI Replacing? The Impact of Generative AI on Online Freelancing Platforms — Hui, Reshef, Zhou (WashU / NYU) — 2023 working paper — https://olin.washu.edu/about/news-and-media/news/2023/08/study-ai-tools-cause-a-decline-in-freelance-work-and-incomeat-least-in-the-short-run.php — study (Upwork data) — A
     After ChatGPT, writing freelancers got fewer jobs (−2%) and lower monthly earnings (−5.2%); effects were larger for experienced, higher-priced freelancers.

[S2] Generative AI and online freelancing (Demirci, Hannane, Zhu) — Management Science; summarised by Brookings "Is generative AI a job killer?" — 2024–2025 — https://www.brookings.edu/articles/is-generative-ai-a-job-killer-evidence-from-the-freelance-market — study — A
     21% fewer job posts for automation-prone writing and coding jobs within eight months of ChatGPT, versus manual-intensive jobs.

[S3] Winners and Losers of Generative AI: Early Evidence of Shifts in Freelancer Demand — Teutloff et al. (Oxford Internet Institute, ETLA and others) — J. Economic Behavior & Organization, 29 Jan 2025 — https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/the-winners-and-losers-of-generative-ai-in-the-freelance-job-market/ — study, 3M+ postings — A
     Substitutable skills fell 20–50% vs trend, sharpest in 1–3 week jobs; ML programming +24%, chatbot development nearly tripled; demand for novices fell.

[S4] Upwork's Future Workforce Index 2026 — Upwork Research Institute — 14 Jul 2026 — https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-future-workforce-index-2026-how-ai-is-redefining-the-value-of-work-as-skilled-freelancing-accelerates — vendor survey + platform data — B (Upwork has an incentive to report freelancing growth)
     AI work earns 34% more per hour on Upwork; complex AI work earnings +45% YoY; low-complexity generative/creative contracts +90% in volume but −13% per contract.

[S5] Upwork Help Center — Freelancer Service Fee — Upwork — current as of 2026 — https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538 — docs — A (for Upwork's own rules)
     Since 1 May 2025 the freelancer fee is variable (0–15%) per contract, shown before you submit a proposal and fixed for the contract's life. Date of change and range confirmed via secondary summaries (golance.com, gigradar.io, C).

## First job, reputation, channels

[S6] Inefficient Hiring in Entry-Level Labor Markets — Amanda Pallais — American Economic Review 104(11), 2014 — https://www.nber.org/papers/w18917 — randomized field experiment on oDesk (now Upwork) — A
     Randomly hiring 952 novices and posting public evaluations raised their later employment, asking wages and earnings; inexperienced workers' earnings roughly tripled after one job.

[S7] Landing the First Job: The Value of Intermediaries in Online Hiring — Stanton & Thomas — Review of Economic Studies 83(2), 2016 — https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/65160/ — study — A
     Agency-affiliated new workers get hired more and paid more early; the edge fades once good independent workers earn public feedback; largest for skilled workers in developing countries.

[S8] How Buyers Buy (professional services) — Hinge Research Institute — 2013 study, updated later editions (2018) — https://hingemarketing.com/uploads/HowBuyersBuy_Executive_Summary.pdf — vendor survey, 822 buyers / 533 providers — B
     71% of buyers first turn to friends and colleagues; most satisfied buyers say they would refer but were never asked; later editions show fewer buyers asking for referrals and more using search.

[S9] B2B Cold Outreach Benchmarks 2025 — Belkins (with Reply.io, Expandi, Nooks) — 2025 — https://belkins.io/resources/b2b-cold-outreach-benchmarks-2025-report — vendor data, 16.5M emails across 93 domains, Jan–Dec 2024 — C (lead-gen agency; method not independently audited)
     Average reply rate 5.8% (2024, down from 6.8%); the first email in a sequence gets the highest reply rate (8.4%); 4+ emails triple unsubscribes and spam complaints.

[S10] Cold email reply rate benchmarks — Instantly.ai — updated 2026 — https://instantly.ai/blog/cold-email-reply-rate-benchmarks — vendor blog — C
     Claims first follow-up adds 40–50% more replies. Contradicts S9 on follow-ups; recorded as a disagreement, not a fact.

## Pricing and contracts

[S11] Incentives versus Transaction Costs: A Theory of Procurement Contracts — Bajari & Tadelis — RAND Journal of Economics 32(3), 2001 — https://ideas.repec.org/a/rje/randje/v32y2001i3p387-407.html — peer-reviewed theory — A
     Fixed-price gives the seller incentives to cut cost but makes every change a costly renegotiation; cost-plus (time-based) wins when the project is complex or the design incomplete.

[S12] Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity — METR — Jul 2025 — https://metr.org/ (summarised at https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jul/12/ai-open-source-productivity/) — RCT, 16 devs, 246 tasks — A
     AI use made tasks take 19% longer; developers predicted 24% faster and afterwards still believed 20% faster.

[S13] We are Changing our Developer Productivity Experiment Design — METR — 24 Feb 2026 — https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/ — research update — A
     Follow-up point estimates moved toward a speed-up but intervals cross zero; METR calls the signal unreliable because developers now refuse to work without AI (selection bias), and believes the true speed-up in early 2026 is larger than in early 2025.

[S14] Legal Trends Report 2024 — Clio — Oct 2024 — https://www.clio.com/about/press/clio-latest-legal-trends-report/ — vendor data from its law-firm customers — B
     Estimates 74% of hourly billable law-firm tasks could be automated by AI; flat-fee bills rose in 2024 (from 19% of bills in 2023); Clio advises moving off hourly billing.

[S15] Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 — Stack Overflow — Jul 2025 (fielded 29 May–23 Jun 2025, 49,009 responses) — https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025 (summarised by TechRepublic, ShiftMag) — survey — B
     46% distrust AI output accuracy vs 33% who trust it; 66% name "almost right, but not quite" AI solutions as a top frustration.

[S16] Scope Patrol (Pulse of the Profession 2018) — Project Management Institute — 2018 — https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/scope-creep-rising-11308 — survey — B
     52% of projects completed in the prior 12 months had scope creep, up from 43% five years earlier.

[S17] SPIN Selling — Neil Rackham / Huthwaite — 1988 book; research 1976–1986 — https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/blog/neil-rackham-research-spin — observational study of 35,000 sales calls — B (large, but proprietary and old)
     In large B2B sales, winners asked about problems and their implications more than they pitched; small-sale closing techniques backfired.

[S18] Common Paper Professional Services Agreement v1.1 — Common Paper — current — https://commonpaper.com/standards/professional-services-agreement/1.1/cover-page-and-standard-terms-docx ; cap guidance https://help.commonpaper.com/en/articles/6901520-general-cap-amount — standard contract, CC BY 4.0, drafted by a committee of lawyers — B
     Free, standard services contract; default liability cap is 1× fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, with optional higher caps for security/privacy breaches.

[S19] The Value of Keeping the Right Customers — Amy Gallo, HBR — 29 Oct 2014 — https://hbr.org/2014/10/the-value-of-keeping-the-right-customers — essay — C for the "5–25×" figure
     Source of the widely repeated "acquiring a customer costs 5–25× more" line; the article names no study for the range. Underlying Reichheld/Sasser work (HBR 1990) covered credit cards and insurance.

## IP law (primary)

[S20] 17 U.S.C. § 101 (definition of "work made for hire") and § 204(a) (transfers need a signed writing) — US Code — current — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/101 — statute — A
     A contractor's work is "for hire" only in nine listed categories with a signed agreement; most custom software is not in them, so the client needs a written assignment.

[S21] Copyright Act, 1957 (India), ss. 17, 19 — Government of India — current — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (search "Copyright Act, 1957") — statute — A
     Employer owns works made under a contract of service; otherwise the author owns them; assignment must be in writing, signed, and specify rights, duration and territory; unexercised rights lapse after one year unless the deed says otherwise (s.19(4)).

## India: FEMA, GST, income tax (all need CA sign-off)

[S22] Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 — RBI, Notification FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB — 13 Jan 2026, in force 1 Oct 2026 — https://rbi.org.in/ScriptS/BS_FemaNotifications.aspx?Id=13277 — regulation — A
     Replaces the 2015 export regulations; principle-based, aimed at easing small exporters' compliance.

[S23] FEMA (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026 — RBI — 22 Sep 2026, gazetted 24 Sep, effective 1 Oct 2026 — summarised by SCC Online (https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/29/rbi-amends-fema-export-import-goods-services-regulations-2026/) and India Briefing — regulation (read via legal-press summary) — A for the rule, B for the summary
     Realisation period cut from 15 to 9 months (12 months if invoiced/settled in INR); for services the clock runs from the invoice date; services exporters file an Export Declaration Form (EDF) with their bank within 30 days of the end of the invoice month.

[S24] Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, s. 2(6) and s. 16 — Government of India — current — https://cbic-gst.gov.in/ (text quoted in AAR WB 02/2017-18, https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/wb_2017-18_02_dated_21032018.pdf) — statute — A
     Five cumulative conditions for "export of services" (supplier in India, recipient outside, place of supply outside, payment in convertible foreign exchange or RBI-permitted INR, not establishments of one person); exports are zero-rated and may be made under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) without paying IGST.

[S25] CGST Act, 2017, s. 2(6) "aggregate turnover"; Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax (13 Oct 2017); CGST Rules r. 96A; Circular 8/8/2017-GST — CBIC — current — https://cbic-gst.gov.in/ — statute/notification — A (read via secondary quotes: taxtmi, incorpx, C)
     Aggregate turnover includes exports; inter-state service suppliers (exports are inter-state) under ₹20 lakh are exempt from compulsory registration; LUT is filed in Form GST RFD-11.

[S26] Income-tax Act, 2025, s. 58 (presumptive income) — Government of India — in force 1 Apr 2026 — https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/ (summaries: taxguru, taxscan, taxtmi) — statute — A for the Act, B for summaries
     Replaces old ss. 44AD/44ADA/44AE; for specified professions, income presumed at 50% of gross receipts; limit ₹50 lakh, or ₹75 lakh if cash receipts ≤5%.

[S27] Income-tax Act, 2025, s. 408 (advance tax, presumptive cases) — Income Tax Department — current — https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-408-6 — statute — A
     A taxpayer declaring income under s. 58 pays all advance tax in one instalment by 15 March.

[S28] RBI Master Direction on Regulation of Payment Aggregators (PA-CB), Oct 2023 circular and Sep 2025 Directions — RBI — summarised by Winvesta / Business Standard — regulation (via summaries) — A for the rule, C for the vendor summary
     Cross-border payment aggregators must be RBI-authorised; per-transaction cap for PA-CB export collections is ₹25 lakh.

[S29] USD/INR close — PTI via The Pioneer — 24 Sep 2026 — https://dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/rupee-falls-12-paise-to-close-at-rs-95-74-against-us-dollar?year=2026 — data (news wire) — B
