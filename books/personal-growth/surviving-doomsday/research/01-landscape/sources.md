## Hiring and employment

[S1] Canaries in the Coal Mine? Six Facts about the Recent Employment Effects of Artificial Intelligence — Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen (Stanford Digital Economy Lab) — 2025-11 version — https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2025/11/CanariesintheCoalMine_Nov25.pdf — study (ADP payroll microdata, working paper) — Grade A
     Ages 22–25 in the most AI-exposed occupations, software developers included, saw ~16% relative employment decline with firm-time controls; older workers in the same jobs grew.

[S2] Canaries, Interest Rates, and Timing — Stanford Digital Economy Lab — 2026-02-09 — https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canaries-interest-rates-and-timinga-more-on-recent-drivers-of-employment-changes-for-young-workers — data note — Grade A
     Interest rates don't explain the exposed-occupation gap (exposed jobs are *less* rate-sensitive); with firm-time fixed effects the AI-linked decline becomes significant only in 2024, so earlier declines are partly other causes.

[S3] No Widespread Displacement, but the AI Employment Gap for Young Workers Has Widened to 19% — Stanford Digital Economy Lab — 2026-08-12 — https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/ — data update — Grade A
     Gap for ages 22–25 in high-exposure jobs widened from 15% (Aug 2025) to 19%; works through reduced hiring, not separations; concentrated where AI use is automative, not augmentative.

[S4] Generative AI as Seniority-Biased Technological Change — Lichtinger & Hosseini Maasoum (Harvard) — 2025 (SSRN 5425555; May 2026 revision) — https://raw.githack.com/s-mahdihosseini/Website/b99fa2962dcc4c165bbc3c41dd283afa99c5ccfb/GenAI_SenBTC_May_2026.pdf — study (62M résumés, 285k firms, working paper) — Grade A
     From 2023Q1, junior employment at AI-adopting firms fell ~9% within six quarters vs non-adopters, via slower hiring; senior employment kept rising; mid-tier graduates hit hardest.

[S5] The Labor Market for Recent College Graduates — Federal Reserve Bank of New York — data as cited 2026 (2024 Census data) — https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/college-labor-market — official statistics — Grade A ★
     Recent-graduate unemployment: computer engineering 7.8%, computer science 7.0%, above the recent-grad average.

[S6] Remote Work Leaves Younger Workers Sidelined — Liberty Street Economics, FRB New York — 2026-06 — https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/06/remote-work-leaves-younger-workers-sidelined/ — study (blog of research paper) — Grade A
     Remote work explains an estimated 64% of the rise in young-graduate unemployment; increase concentrated in remotable occupations including software engineering; timing predates generative AI.

[S7] Weak Hiring Is Hurting Young Workers More than AI, Study Says (reporting on St. Louis Fed analysis) — Bloomberg Law — 2026 — https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/weak-hiring-is-hurting-young-workers-more-than-ai-study-says — reporting on official research — Grade B ★
     Most of the 2023–2025 rise in 18–24 unemployment came from fewer openings; a minority (~1.1 of 2.9 points by one reading) traced to shifting demand toward AI skills.

[S8] Evaluating the Impact of AI on the Labor Market (monthly update) — The Budget Lab at Yale — 2026 updates — https://budgetlab.yale.edu/node/1419/pdf — study — Grade A
     No discernible economy-wide disruption since ChatGPT; occupational-mix change predates generative AI; exposure/usage measures unrelated to employment changes so far.

[S9] Large Language Models, Small Labor Market Effects — Humlum & Vestergaard — 2025 (NBER w33777) — https://ideas.repec.org/p/nbr/nberwo/33777.html — study (Danish admin data, 25k workers) — Grade A
     Precise null effects on earnings and hours two years after chatbot adoption; ~3% time savings; new oversight tasks absorb gains.

[S10] Software Development Job Postings on Indeed in the United States (IHLIDXUSTPSOFTDEVE) — Indeed Hiring Lab via FRED — series through 2026-06 — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/?g=1WIe6 — data — Grade A ★

     Software postings up ~15% since Feb 2025 vs −7% overall; 71% of the May 2025–May 2026 increase was senior roles; vs Jan 2025, senior +13.5%, mid −6.7%, entry −6.3%.

## Productivity studies

[S18] Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity — Becker, Rush et al. (METR) — 2025-07-10 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089 — RCT — Grade A
     16 experienced maintainers, 246 tasks in their own large repos: AI allowed → 19% slower (CI +2% to +39%); they forecast 24% faster and afterwards believed 20% faster.

[S19] We are Changing our Developer Productivity Experiment Design — METR — 2026-02-24 — https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/ — RCT follow-up — Grade A
     Late-2025 rerun: returning devs ~18% faster (CI −38% to +9%), new recruits ~4% faster (CI −15% to +9%); many devs refused to work without AI, so selection bias likely understates speedup.

[S20] The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers — Cui, Demirer, Jaffe, Musolff, Peng, Salz — 2024/2025 — https://www.economics.utoronto.ca/index.php/index/research/downloadSeminarFile/16038 — field RCTs (4,867 devs, Microsoft, Accenture, Fortune 100) — Grade A
     Copilot access → +26.08% completed tasks (SE 10.3%); larger gains for less-experienced/junior developers; adoption only 60–75%.

[S21] The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot — Peng, Kalliamvakou, Cihon, Demirer — 2023 — https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-impact-of-ai-on-developer-productivity-evidence-from-github-copilot — lab RCT (95 devs) *(incentive: Microsoft/GitHub authors)* — Grade A
     Greenfield task (HTTP server in JS) done 55.8% faster with Copilot.

[S22] 2025 DORA State of AI-assisted Software Development — Google Cloud DORA — 2025-09 — https://research.google/pubs/dora-2025-state-of-ai-assisted-software-development-report/ — survey (~5,000 professionals) *(incentive: Google sells AI tools)* — Grade B
     AI adoption now correlates with higher delivery throughput *and* higher instability (change failures, rework); AI "amplifies" existing org strengths and dysfunctions.

[S23] Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 — Stack Overflow — 2025-07 — https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/work — survey (self-report) — Grade A
     84% use or plan to use AI; 46% distrust accuracy vs 33% trust; 66% cite "almost right" output; 72% say vibe coding isn't part of professional work; engineering-manager median pay US $200k vs India $52k.

## What agents can do

[S24] Time Horizon 1.1 — METR — 2026-01-29 — https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/ — benchmark study — Grade A ★
     50%-success task horizon doubling every ~131 days post-2023 (vs ~212 days 2019–2025); frontier agents in early 2026 rated at ~12+ hours on the 50% metric.

[S25] Why we no longer evaluate SWE-bench Verified — OpenAI — 2026-02 — https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/ — docs *(incentive: lab)* — Grade A
     Audit: 59.4% of 138 hard-failed Verified tasks were flawed; evidence of training-data leakage; one model scored 80.9% Verified vs 45.9% on SWE-bench Pro.

[S26] Coding Agents Have Converged: Why the SWE-bench Leaderboard Can No Longer Order Its Top Entries — arXiv 2609.17394 — 2026-09 — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.17394 — preprint — Grade B
     Top agents solve the same 285/500 Verified tasks and fail the same 51; scaffold choice moves scores up to 29.8 points; adjacent top-30 entries are statistically indistinguishable.

[S27] Anthropic Economic Index: AI's Impact on Software Development — Anthropic — 2025-04 — https://www.anthropic.com/research/impact-software-development — usage data (500k coding interactions) *(incentive: lab)* — Grade A
     79% of Claude Code conversations classified as automation vs 49% on Claude.ai.

[S28] Migrating Code At Scale With LLMs At Google — Google (FSE 2025 industry track) — 2025 — https://conf.researchr.org/details/fse-2025/fse-2025-industry-papers/14/Migrating-Code-At-Scale-With-LLMs-At-Google — deployment case study — Grade A
     39 migrations, 595 changes: ~74% of code changes LLM-generated; devs estimated ~50% less total time; needed AST discovery, build/test validation, and repair loops.

[S29] Anthropic Economic Index — India country brief — Anthropic — 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/research/india-brief-economic-index — usage data *(incentive: lab)* — Grade A ★
     India is Claude's #2 market by share; ~50% of Indian use is technical vs ~30% globally; web-app build/debug is the top use.

## Historical analogues and value shift

[S30] Toil and Technology — James Bessen, IMF Finance & Development — 2015-03 — https://conversableeconomist.com/2015/03/03/atms-and-a-rising-number-of-bank-tellers/ (summary with quotes) — essay with data — Grade B
     ATMs cut tellers per branch from ~20 to ~13 (1988–2004), branches rose ~43% in urban areas, teller employment held steady and the job shifted to relationship/sales work.

[S31] Expertise — David Autor & Neil Thompson, Journal of the European Economic Association 23(4) — 2025 — https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:oup:jeurec:v:23:y:2025:i:4:p:1203-1271 — peer-reviewed study — Grade A
     Automating an occupation's *inexpert* tasks raises wages, cuts employment; automating its *expert* tasks does the reverse. Bookkeepers: employment −⅓, real wages +~40% (1980–2018).

[S32] How the electronic spreadsheet revolutionized business (Planet Money) — NPR — 2015-02-27 — https://www.wfae.org/2015-02-27/how-the-electronic-spreadsheet-revolutionized-business — reporting on Census data — Grade B
     Since 1980, ~400,000 fewer bookkeeping/accounting clerks and ~600,000 more accountants/auditors in the US.

[S33] Occupational Outlook Handbook: Drafters — BLS — 2025 — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/drafters.htm — official statistics — Grade A
     Little or no change projected 2024–34; CAD/BIM let engineers and architects do drafting themselves.

[S34] Beyond the Buzz: Developing the AI Skills Employers Actually Need — Lightcast — 2025-07-23 — https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/beyond-the-buzz-press-release-2025-07-23 — job-posting analysis (1.3B postings) *(incentive: data vendor)* — Grade B
     Postings listing AI skills advertise 28% higher pay (43% with two+ AI skills); 51% of AI-skill postings are outside IT/CS.

[S35] Jobs on the Rise 2026 / Grad's Guide — LinkedIn — 2026-01 — https://letsdatascience.com/news/linkedin-identifies-ai-engineer-as-fastest-growing-role-e82f85f1 — platform data *(incentive)* — Grade B ★
     AI engineer is the fastest-growing US title; postings +143% YoY in 2025; India AI engineering roles +51%/yr.

[S36] Forward-deployed engineer demand — Indeed data via Business Insider; Bloomberry analysis of 1,000 FDE postings — 2026 — https://www.paraform.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-demand-quadrupled ; https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-1000-forward-deployed-engineer-jobs/ — posting analysis *(incentive: recruiter)* — Grade C ★
     FDE postings on Indeed 643 (Apr 2025) → 5,330 (Apr 2026); disclosed median ~$174k.

[S37] AI engineer compensation trends — Levels.fyi — 2025-Q3 / 2026 — https://www.levels.fyi/blog/ai-engineer-compensation-trends-q3-2025.html — self-reported pay data — Grade B ★
     AI/ML premium over non-AI SWE ~6% at entry, widening to 78%+ at staff/principal; L3 premium ~1.34× (late 2024) → ~1.29× (mid 2026): plateauing.
