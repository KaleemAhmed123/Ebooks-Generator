## Validation

[S19] A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial — Camuffo, Cordova, Gambardella, Spina — Management Science, 2020 — https://cepr.org/publications/DP12421 — RCT, 116 startups — A
     Startups trained to state hypotheses and test them with customers earned more, pivoted more, and dropped false-positive ideas faster than a control group given the same feedback training.

[S20] The lean startup method: early-stage teams and hypothesis-based probing — Leatherbee, Katila — Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal, 2020 — https://web.stanford.edu/~rkatila/new/pdf/KatilaLeanStartup2020.pdf — longitudinal, 152 NSF I-Corps teams — A (observational)
     Customer interviews ("probing") drive convergence on an idea and generate new hypotheses; more engagement related to better 18-month performance (post hoc, not causal).
