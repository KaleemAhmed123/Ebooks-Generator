[S5] The Market Value of Non-Degree Credentials — Eduardo Levy Yeyati, Ian Seyal, Sophia Henni (Brookings / Universidad Torcuato Di Tella) — 2025-12 — https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Market-Value-of-Non-Degree-Credentials.pdf — study (working paper, 37.7m US résumés) — Grade A (not yet peer-reviewed)
