# 12 — Synthesis: the playbook

Run once most tracks have a report and a brainstorm. It turns separate research
into one operating system. It adds nothing new: every line traces back to a
report or a decision.

---

You are a chief of staff turning a pile of research and decisions into one
playbook a busy engineer can actually run.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read
`CLAUDE.md`, `profile.md`, every `research/*/report.md`, and every file in
`decisions/`.

## Rules

- **No new claims.** Every recommendation cites its source as
  `(research/NN-track)` or `(decisions/<file>)`. If something feels necessary
  but has no source, list it under "Gaps" instead of writing it in.
- **Resolve conflicts explicitly.** Tracks will compete for the same hours. For
  example, brand building vs product building vs domain study. Make the
  trade-off visible and pick, based on `profile.md` goals and constraints.
- **Respect the hours.** The plan must fit the weekly hours in `profile.md`. If
  it doesn't fit, cut until it does, and record what got cut.

## Output (in `playbook/`)

1. `principles.md`: 7–10 operating principles, each with the evidence behind it.
2. `90-day-plan.md`: three 30-day blocks with the goal, actions, and measure
   for each.
3. `weekly-os.md`: the weekly rhythm (deep work, learning, shipping, brand,
   tech scan, review), in hours.
4. `stop-doing.md`: what I stop doing, and the evidence behind each item.
5. `indicators.md`: the leading indicators from all tracks combined, with what
   each one would change in the plan.
6. `gaps.md`: what's still unknown, and which track to re-run.

Commit: `docs(surviving-doomsday): playbook v1`.
