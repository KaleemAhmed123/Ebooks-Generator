# 10 — Focus and outer noise

The noise has two layers: attention noise (feeds, notifications, constant
switching) and discourse noise (AI doom and hype, timeline predictions, "you're
already too late" posts). Both cost hours and, worse, decision quality.

---

You are a researcher in attention and behavior change who also builds software.
Research how engineers protect focus and keep a steady head while the AI
discourse runs hot.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md`, especially the
attention section. Use web search, and prefer peer-reviewed research.

## Questions to answer

1. What does research say about task switching, interruptions, and recovery
   time for knowledge work? Quote the actual findings, not the folklore numbers
   that circulate online.
2. Which interventions have evidence? Notification batching, app limits,
   environment design, time-blocking, implementation intentions, habit
   formation. Include effect sizes where they exist.
3. **Information diet**: how do you stay informed about AI without drowning?
   Design a small, high-signal input set and a cadence for it. This connects to
   the tech radar track.
4. **AI anxiety**: what does research say about job-insecurity stress and how it
   affects performance and decisions? Which coping strategies have evidence
   behind them? Keep it practical, and tell me where professional help applies.
5. How do you tell useful urgency (the market really is moving) from manufactured
   urgency (engagement-driven fear)?
6. Deep-work claims: which are supported and which are productivity-guru lore?

## Avoid

- Dopamine-detox pseudoscience, except under "Myths and hype to ignore".
- Moralizing about phone use.

## Output

Write `research/10-focus/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`. In
"What to do", include my personal noise policy: allowed inputs, the cadence for
each, blocked inputs, and a weekly attention audit that takes 15 minutes.
Commit: `research(surviving-doomsday/10-focus): first pass`.
