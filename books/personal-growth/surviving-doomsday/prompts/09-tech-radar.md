# 09 — Tech radar: picking up new tech before others

The goal is to be early on technology that lasts, without chasing every launch.
This track builds a personal system for spotting, testing, and adopting new
tech. Re-run it quarterly.

---

You are a principal engineer who runs a technology radar for a large org. You've
been early on several tools that lasted and burned out on several that didn't.
Research how to spot durable technology early, and build me a personal radar.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md` and
`research/01-landscape/report.md` if they exist. Use web search and primary
sources: release notes, changelogs, papers, official docs, and download or
usage statistics.

## Questions to answer

1. How do established radars work? Look at the ThoughtWorks Technology Radar's
   adopt/trial/assess/hold method and how they decide placements. What can a
   single person borrow from that?
2. Which signals predict that a technology will last vs fade? Consider backing,
   adoption curves, ecosystem growth, standards bodies, maintainer health, and
   real production usage. Use historical cases on both sides.
3. Where does new tech show up first, before mainstream coverage? Papers,
   changelogs, specific communities and newsletters, conference talks. Rank the
   sources by signal-to-noise, with reasons.
4. How do you evaluate fast? Design a time-boxed spike protocol (2–8 hours) with
   a written verdict.
5. **Current snapshot**: as of today, what's actually moving in AI engineering?
   Agent frameworks and protocols like MCP, evals, inference, model releases,
   coding agents. Place 15–25 items into adopt/trial/assess/hold *for my
   profile*, with reasons and sources.
6. How do you turn being early into career value? Writing about it, building
   with it, and contributing to it.

## Avoid

- Hype-cycle charts presented as evidence.
- GitHub stars as the only signal.
- A snapshot without dates. Date every placement.

## Output

Write `research/09-tech-radar/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`. Also
add `research/09-tech-radar/radar.md`, the dated adopt/trial/assess/hold table,
which future runs update rather than rewrite. In "What to do", include a weekly
scan routine that takes under 90 minutes. Commit:
`research(surviving-doomsday/09-tech-radar): <date> pass`.
