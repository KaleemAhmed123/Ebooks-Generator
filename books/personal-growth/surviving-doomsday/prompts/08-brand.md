# 08 — Personal brand

For an engineer, a personal brand is proof of work that strangers can find. This
track covers which channels earn real opportunities, and how to stand out when
AI-generated content is flooding every feed.

---

You are an engineer who built an audience that pays: jobs, clients, and product
customers, not vanity followers. You are also skeptical of the creator economy.
Research how engineers build a reputation that turns into opportunities.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md`. Use web search.
Platform algorithm claims are C-grade unless the platform published them.

## Questions to answer

1. What's the evidence that public work (open source, writing, talks, building
   in public) leads to jobs, clients, or customers for engineers? Look for
   hiring-manager and recruiter surveys, and research on open-source
   contribution and careers.
2. Compare channels for an engineer: GitHub, a personal blog or newsletter,
   LinkedIn, X, YouTube, niche communities, conference talks. For each, what is
   it good for, what does it cost in time, and how long until it pays off?
3. **The AI flood**: AI-generated content has made posts cheap. What still cuts
   through? Look for evidence on originality, specificity, real artifacts, and
   strong opinions backed by work.
4. Positioning: how narrow should the niche be? How do you pick a "known for"
   that fits `profile.md` and the domain track?
5. Which metrics matter? Inbound opportunities, replies from the people I want
   to reach, and conversion to paid work. Which are vanity: followers and
   impressions?
6. Risks: time sink, burnout, reputational blowups, platform dependence. How do
   you own the audience? Email, a personal domain.

## Avoid

- Growth-hacking playbooks and engagement bait.
- Advice from people whose only product is teaching personal branding, without
  heavy discounting.

## Output

Write `research/08-brand/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`. In
"What to do", include a minimum viable brand system that takes 3–4 hours a week:
what to publish, where, the cadence, and the one metric to track. Commit:
`research(surviving-doomsday/08-brand): first pass`.
