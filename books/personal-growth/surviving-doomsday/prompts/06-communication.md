# 06 — Communication

When everyone can generate polished prose, clear thinking on paper and spoken
trust become the signal. This track covers writing, explaining, and persuading
for engineers.

---

You are an engineering director who has read thousands of design docs and sat
through thousands of stakeholder meetings. Research what makes engineers'
communication effective, and what changes when AI writes everyone's first draft.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md`. Use web search,
plus primary sources like company engineering handbooks and published writing
guides from engineering orgs.

## Questions to answer

1. Which kinds of engineering writing carry the most career weight? Design docs,
   RFCs, postmortems, proposals, status updates. What do respected orgs' own
   guides say good looks like?
2. How do you explain technical trade-offs to non-technical decision makers?
   Look for structures with evidence behind them, such as bottom-line-up-front
   and pyramid-style writing, and how execs actually read.
3. **AI-era signal**: what does research or credible reporting say about how
   readers perceive AI-written text, and the "AI slop" penalty? What still
   signals a real human with real judgement?
4. Spoken communication: meetings, demos, and technical interviews. Which
   habits actually change outcomes, and which are just image management?
5. Persuasion and negotiation basics for engineers: salary, scope, and pushing
   back on stakeholders. Use negotiation research, not tactics porn.
6. If `profile.md` says English isn't my first language or I sell across
   cultures, research how to write and speak clearly for an international
   audience.

## Avoid

- Generic "be a good communicator" advice.
- Templates with no reasoning behind them.

## Output

Write `research/06-communication/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`.
In "What to do", include a writing practice: what to write weekly, how to get
feedback, and a checklist for one-page design docs. Commit:
`research(surviving-doomsday/06-communication): first pass`.
