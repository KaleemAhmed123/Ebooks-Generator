# 07 — Dealing with clients

Freelance and consulting work is how many engineers diversify away from a single
employer. This track covers finding clients, scoping, pricing, delivering, and
keeping them, now that AI changes how fast work gets done.

---

You are an independent consultant who has run a profitable software practice
for a decade and has seen the AI shift change pricing. Research how engineers
win and keep clients now.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md` and
`research/01-landscape/report.md` if they exist. Use web search. Freelance
platform data and surveys count as B-grade at best. Say who ran the survey.

## Questions to answer

1. Where do good clients actually come from for engineers right now? Referrals,
   platforms, content, outbound, partnerships. What does the data say about each
   channel's quality, not just its volume? How are freelance platforms changing
   with AI?
2. **Pricing when AI makes you faster**: hourly vs fixed vs value-based vs
   retainer. If AI makes me twice as fast, why does hourly billing hurt me,
   and how do practitioners reprice? Grade the evidence.
3. Discovery and scoping: how to run a discovery call, write a proposal, and
   control scope. What contract basics protect an engineer? Cover payment terms,
   IP, liability caps, and change requests. Point to primary sources, not
   lawyer-blog summaries, where possible.
4. Managing expectations: clients who saw an AI demo and expect magic. How do
   you set expectations about AI-built deliverables, quality, and maintenance?
5. Retention and expansion: turning projects into retainers, and when to fire
   a client.
6. Based on `profile.md`: what's practical for cross-border work from my
   location? Payment rails, invoicing, taxes, time zones. Use official sources
   only, and mark anything that needs a professional's sign-off.

## Avoid

- "Just charge more" advice without mechanism.
- Platform-specific hacks that age in weeks.

## Output

Write `research/07-clients/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`. Also add

one-page proposal skeleton, and a pricing decision tree. Commit:
`research(surviving-doomsday/07-clients): first pass`.
