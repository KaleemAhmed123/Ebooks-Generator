# 04 — Thinking quality (the "IQ" track)

The original brief said "IQ". The evidence for raising IQ itself is weak, so
this track goes after what actually raises effective reasoning. It confirms or
refutes that reframe first.

---

You are a cognitive scientist who is allergic to brain-training marketing.
Research what measurably improves an adult engineer's reasoning and learning,
and what AI does to both.

Work in `books/personal-growth/surviving-doomsday/` in this repo. Read its
`CLAUDE.md` first and follow it exactly. Then read `profile.md`. Use web search,
and favor meta-analyses and systematic reviews over single studies.

## Questions to answer

1. **Check the reframe first.** What does the evidence say about raising adult
   IQ or fluid intelligence? Look at meta-analyses on working-memory and
   brain-training transfer, the FTC's 2016 case against Lumosity, and education
   effects. If the evidence is better than expected, say so plainly.
2. What does improve effective reasoning in a field? Domain knowledge, retrieval
   practice, spaced repetition, worked examples, writing to think, explaining to
   others. Give effect sizes where they exist.
3. What do sleep, exercise, and stress do to cognitive performance? Report only
   well-supported effects, with the size of the effect.
4. **AI and thinking**: what do 2024–2026 studies say about cognitive
   offloading, critical thinking, and learning outcomes when people use LLMs?
   When does AI make you sharper (a Socratic tutor, a sparring partner, fast
   feedback), and when does it make you weaker (outsourcing the struggle)?
5. Which mental models and reasoning tools have real support for engineers?
   Examples: first-principles decomposition, Fermi estimation, inversion,
   thinking in systems. Flag the ones that are only popular.

## Avoid

- Nootropics, "10x your brain", and productivity-influencer claims, except
  under "Myths and hype to ignore".
- Overstating single small studies.

## Output

Write `research/04-thinking/report.md` and `sources.md` per `CLAUDE.md`. In
"What to do", include a set of rules for using AI without losing the skill,
plus a learning protocol for picking up a new technical field in 30 days.
Commit: `research(surviving-doomsday/04-thinking): first pass`.
