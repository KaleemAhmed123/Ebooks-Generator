### Querying

```text
{service="orders"} |= "error"
{service="orders"} | json | status >= 500
sum by (service) (rate({job="containers"} |= "ERROR" [5m]))
```

### Log in JSON

```ts
console.log(JSON.stringify({
  level: "error", msg: "payment failed",
  orderId: "8812", requestId: req.id, ts: new Date().toISOString(),
}));
```

- `| json` then makes every field filterable. Unstructured lines force substring matching, which finds the wrong things
