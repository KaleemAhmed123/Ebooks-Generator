### Domain and account hygiene

- Auto-renew on the domain, with a payment method that does not expire
- Provider billing alerts, so a failed payment is noticed before suspension
- More than one person able to reach every account

### The honest position

- A single VPS has a failure mode that no amount of preparation removes: the whole thing is one machine. Recovery is fast, not instant
- If minutes of downtime are unacceptable, the answer is a second machine, and that is a different architecture
