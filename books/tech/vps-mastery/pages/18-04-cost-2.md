### Reducing it honestly

- Serve images from object storage with a CDN in front, not from the VPS
- Set an alert on bandwidth in the provider panel, not just on the total bill
- Delete snapshots on a schedule
- Annual billing is often 10 to 20% cheaper. Only worth it once the provider has been used for a few months

### What is worth paying for

- **Off-site backups.** Two dollars a month against losing the business
- **Object storage.** It removes the largest thing from the backup and survives the box
- **A bigger box.** Engineering time to fit a stack into 4 GB costs more than 8 GB does
