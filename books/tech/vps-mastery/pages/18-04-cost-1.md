## Cost

- A realistic monthly bill for the stack in Part Four, as of 2026

| Item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| 8 GB VPS, 4 cores, 160 GB disk | $18 to $30 |
| Domain | $1 |
| TLS | $0 |
| Backup storage, 100 GB at a second provider | $0.60 to $2 |
| Object storage for uploads, 200 GB | $1 to $4 |
| Uptime monitoring | $0, self-hosted |
| **Total** | **$21 to $37** |

- The same stack on a managed platform with a managed database and equivalent bandwidth typically lands between $150 and $400

### Where the money actually goes wrong

| Surprise | Cause |
|---|---|
| Bandwidth overage | Serving video or large images from the box. Page 10-11 |
| A TURN relay | Every relayed call is real traffic. Page 10-10 |
| Snapshots kept forever | Charged per gigabyte, per month, silently |
| A second box left running after a drill | The most common one |
| Egress from object storage | Cloudflare R2 charges none. Most others do |
