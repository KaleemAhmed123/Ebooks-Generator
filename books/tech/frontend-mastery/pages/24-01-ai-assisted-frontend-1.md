# Module 24: AI-Assisted Frontend

## AI-Assisted Engineering Workflows

In 2023, the industry debated whether AI would replace developers. By 2026, the debate is over: AI has not replaced engineers, but **engineers who use AI have replaced engineers who do not.**

The role of a Senior Frontend Engineer has shifted fundamentally. You are no longer judged on how fast you can type out a React component or configure a Redux store. You are judged on your ability to orchestrate, audit, and securely integrate AI-generated code. You have transitioned from a *Code Writer* to a *Code Editor* and *System Architect*.

### From Scaffolding to Orchestration

Early AI tools (like the first iteration of GitHub Copilot) were essentially advanced autocomplete. They saved you keystrokes.

Modern AI workflows involve autonomous coding agents. You provide a prompt ("Refactor this React Context to use Zustand, ensure it persists to localStorage, and write Playwright tests for the new edge cases"), and the agent writes 400 lines of code across 6 different files in 10 seconds.

Your job is no longer writing the code. Your job is **Context Engineering** and **Auditing**.
