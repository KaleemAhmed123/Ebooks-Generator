### The rest of the map

| Where | Law | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| EU | European Accessibility Act | EN 301 549, WCAG 2.1 AA |
| United States, public sector | Section 508 | WCAG 2.0 AA, moving to 2.1 |
| United States, private sector | ADA Title III, established by case law | courts have generally applied WCAG 2.1 AA |
| United Kingdom | Equality Act 2010, public sector regulations | WCAG 2.2 AA |
| Canada | Accessible Canada Act, AODA in Ontario | WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 AA |

The United States has no statutory web standard for private companies, which is
worse rather than better: exposure comes through lawsuits, and thousands of ADA
web accessibility cases are filed there every year.

**The practical answer is to build to WCAG 2.2 AA.** It is a superset of 2.1 AA,
so it satisfies every row in that table at once, and 2.2 is what the UK already
requires.
