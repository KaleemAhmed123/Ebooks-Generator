## Accessibility and the Law

Everything else in this module is a technique. This page is why the budget for
those techniques exists.

Accessibility used to be argued for on ethics and market size, and it lost those
arguments in a lot of planning meetings. Since June 2025 it is a compliance
requirement with a fine attached, which is a different conversation.

### The European Accessibility Act

The EAA became enforceable on **28 June 2025** across all 27 EU member states.

**Who it covers.** E-commerce sellers, banking, transport ticketing, e-books,
and telecoms, with at least **10 employees** and **2 million euro** in annual
turnover or balance sheet total. Micro-enterprises below that are exempt.

**The part teams outside Europe miss:** it applies to companies **based anywhere
that sell products or services into the EU**. A US shop that ships to Germany is
in scope.

**The standard** is EN 301 549, the European standard for ICT accessibility,
which incorporates **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

**The penalty** reaches **100,000 euro or 4% of annual revenue**, set per member
state.

### It is being enforced

This is not a law that passed and then sat there. Through the second half of
2025 and into 2026:

- **France** issued formal legal notices to major retailers in July
- **Sweden** opened market surveillance in October
- The **first EAA lawsuits** were filed in French commercial court in November
- The **Dutch ACM** is actively enforcing against e-commerce and electronic
  communications

That timeline is why "strict accessibility" started appearing in job
descriptions as a requirement rather than a value.
