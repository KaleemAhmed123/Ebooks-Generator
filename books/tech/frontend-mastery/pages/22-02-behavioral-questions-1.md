## The Behavioral Round (STAR Method)

Even if you write perfect React code and crush the System Design round, you will fail the interview if you bomb the Behavioral Round.

Companies do not want to hire "Brilliant Jerks." They want to hire developers who can communicate, take feedback, and de-escalate conflict.

### The Questions
You will inevitably be asked:
- *"Tell me about a time you disagreed with a Senior Engineer."*
- *"Tell me about a time you failed to meet a deadline."*
- *"Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly."*

### The Anti-Pattern: Rambling
Junior developers answer these questions by rambling for 5 minutes, giving too much backstory, and forgetting what the actual question was.
