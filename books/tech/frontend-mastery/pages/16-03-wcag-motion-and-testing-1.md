## WCAG, Motion, and Testing Accessibility

ARIA and focus management are the mechanics. WCAG is the standard that says which mechanics you owe the user, and it is the one that appears in contracts and procurement documents.

### What WCAG actually is

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines organize every requirement under four principles, remembered as **POUR**.

- **Perceivable.** The user can sense the content. Text alternatives for images, captions for video, sufficient color contrast.
- **Operable.** The user can drive the interface. Everything reachable by keyboard, no keyboard traps, enough time to complete tasks.
- **Understandable.** The interface behaves predictably. Consistent navigation, clear labels, errors that say how to fix them.
- **Robust.** Assistive technology can parse it. Valid markup, correct roles and names.

Each criterion has a level: A, AA, or AAA. **AA is the target**, because it is what the European Accessibility Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act case law, and virtually every public sector contract require. AAA is aspirational and not achievable for all content.
