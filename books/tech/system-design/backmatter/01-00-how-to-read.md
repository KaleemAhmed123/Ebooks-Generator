# Glossary

## How to read it

- 386 terms, alphabetical. Every one of them is defined somewhere in the six booklets, and every entry here is one line
- The last column says **where the term is introduced**, so one line is never the end of the answer

### The pointer

`5 · 8-06` means **booklet 5, module 8, its 6th page**. Punctuation and case are ignored in the ordering, so `B-tree` files under B and `p50` under P.

| # | Booklet | What it owns |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations | reliability, latency, idempotency, retries, timeouts |
| 2 | Data | storage engines, indexes, replication, partitioning |
| 3 | Consistency | isolation, transactions, consensus, locks, clocks |
| 4 | Events | brokers, delivery, streams, event-driven design |
| 5 | Services | boundaries, gateways, caching, limits, geography |
| 6 | Case Studies | eighteen designs, worked end to end |

- **One term is defined once.** Where a booklet uses an idea another one owns, it points rather than repeats — so the pointer here is the page that explains it, not the last page that mentioned it
- A **bold term** inside a definition has an entry of its own
