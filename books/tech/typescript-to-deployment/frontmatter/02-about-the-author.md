# About the Author

**Kaleem Ahmed** is a backend engineer working across Node.js, TypeScript, Python, AWS and Next.js. He has built and shipped a multi-vendor marketplace for personalized products, a couples app designed around a shared AI, a humanitarian operations portal coordinating university placements, a voice-first marketplace connecting buyers and suppliers, and an AI document automation platform.

He treats AI as a tool that multiplies output and never as a substitute for ownership. Every line still gets reviewed, every irreversible action still needs a human, and the engineer who merges the change still answers for it. Booklet 9 is that view written down.

Away from code he works through algorithmic problems, with more than 1,500 solved, a peak LeetCode rating of 1798 and a global rank of 812 in Weekly Contest 359.

This series is what he wishes had existed when he was learning: the concept, the smallest example that proves it, and the failure mode nobody mentions until it happens to you.
