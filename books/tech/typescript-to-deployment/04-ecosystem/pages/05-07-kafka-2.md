### The concepts that decide whether it fits

- A **topic** is split into **partitions**, and partitions are the unit of parallelism. Ten partitions means at most ten consumers in a group
- **Ordering is per partition, not per topic.** The message key decides the partition, so keying by `sellerId` keeps one seller's events in order
- A **consumer group** shares the work. Each partition goes to exactly one member of a group
- An **offset** is a consumer's position. Committing it is what makes progress durable, and it is why replay is just moving the offset back

### Kafka or RabbitMQ

- **Kafka** for high volume, replay, event sourcing, and feeding analytics. Weak at per-message retry and delayed delivery
- **RabbitMQ** for routing, per-message acknowledgement, dead letter queues and priorities. Not built to retain history
- Kafka is heavier to operate. Reach for it when replay or throughput is the actual requirement
