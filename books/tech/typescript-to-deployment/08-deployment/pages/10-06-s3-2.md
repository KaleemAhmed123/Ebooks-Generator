### Storage classes

| Class | Costs | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | most | anything read regularly |
| Intelligent-Tiering | a small monitoring fee | **unknown access patterns. A safe default** |
| Standard-IA | less storage, a retrieval fee | backups, older uploads |
| Glacier Flexible | very little | archives, minutes to hours to restore |

```bash
aws s3api put-bucket-lifecycle-configuration --bucket acme-uploads \
  --lifecycle-configuration file://lifecycle.json
```

- **A lifecycle rule moving objects to Intelligent-Tiering after 30 days is usually a large saving for one command**
- Add a rule to expire incomplete multipart uploads after 7 days. They are invisible in the console and billed
