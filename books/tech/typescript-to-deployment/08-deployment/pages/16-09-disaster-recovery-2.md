### The failure nobody plans for

- **The account itself.** A compromised root, a billing suspension, or a deletion that replicates everywhere
- **Backups in a separate account with a separate root** is the only defence, and it is cheap

### The test

- **Restore into a different region once a year and time it.** An untested plan is a document
- Write the measured number next to the target. If they disagree, one of them has to change
