### The fanout pattern

- **SNS topic in front, an SQS queue per consumer behind.** Each consumer gets its own copy, its own retry behavior and its own dead letter queue
- A slow consumer then backs up its own queue and affects nobody else, which is the whole point
- **This is the standard event-driven shape on AWS**, and it is worth defaulting to over subscribing services directly

```bash
aws sns set-subscription-attributes --subscription-arn <sub> \
  --attribute-name FilterPolicy \
  --attribute-value '{"eventType":["order.created","order.cancelled"]}'
```

- **A filter policy means a subscriber only receives what it cares about**, filtered before delivery rather than after

### The details

- **`RawMessageDelivery: true`**, or the SQS body arrives wrapped in an SNS envelope you have to unwrap
- Other protocols: HTTPS endpoints, email, SMS, and Lambda
- **An HTTPS subscription must confirm itself.** Your endpoint has to handle `SubscriptionConfirmation` or it never receives anything
- **Verify the message signature** on an HTTPS subscription, exactly as Booklet 6 says for any webhook
