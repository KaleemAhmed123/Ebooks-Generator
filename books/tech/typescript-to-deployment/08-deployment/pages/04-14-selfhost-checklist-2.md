### Upgrades

- [ ] Someone knows how each major upgrade is done
- [ ] Release notes are read before a major version changes
- [ ] A snapshot is taken before any upgrade

### The exit plan

- [ ] **You know how to move each service to managed**, and roughly what it costs
- [ ] Nothing depends on a local filesystem path that a second machine would not have

- **The last two lines are the ones people skip**, and they are what makes growing out of one box a migration rather than a rewrite
