### RDS or Aurora

- **Aurora** is AWS's reimplementation of the storage layer: faster failover, up to fifteen replicas, and storage that grows by itself
- **Aurora Serverless v2** scales capacity with load, which suits spiky or unpredictable traffic
- **Plain RDS is cheaper and completely sufficient** for most services. Aurora earns its cost at high read volume or when sub-minute failover matters

### The parameter to change on day one

- `log_min_duration_statement = 1000` logs every query over a second, which is how a slow query is found before a customer finds it
