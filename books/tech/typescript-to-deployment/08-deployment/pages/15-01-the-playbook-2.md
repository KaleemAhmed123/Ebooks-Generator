### What makes one usable

| Property | Means |
|---|---|
| **copy-pasteable commands** | not "check the database", but the exact query |
| **decision points named** | "if X, do A. If Y, do B" |
| **no prose** | a numbered list. Nobody reads paragraphs during an incident |
| **an escalation line** | who to wake, and when |
| **updated after every use** | the step that was wrong gets fixed |

### The three playbooks that matter most

- **Deploy.** Run so often it becomes muscle memory, which is the point
- **Rollback.** Rehearsed, so it is not being learned during the incident
- **The first ten minutes.** What to check, in what order, before anyone starts guessing

- **A playbook nobody has followed is fiction.** Walk each one on a quiet afternoon before you need it
