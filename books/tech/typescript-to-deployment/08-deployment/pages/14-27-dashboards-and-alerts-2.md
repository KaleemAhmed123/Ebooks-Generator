### The rules

- **Alert on symptoms, not causes.** "Users are getting errors" is actionable; "CPU is 80 percent" often is not
- **Every alert names an action.** If the response is to look and do nothing, it is a dashboard panel, not an alert
- **Page only for what needs a human now.** Everything else goes to a channel and waits for morning
- **An alert that fires weekly and is ignored is worse than no alert**, because it trains people to ignore the next one too
