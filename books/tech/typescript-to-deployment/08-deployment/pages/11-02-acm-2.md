### Attaching it

```bash
aws elbv2 create-listener --load-balancer-arn <alb> \
  --protocol HTTPS --port 443 \
  --certificates CertificateArn=<arn> \
  --ssl-policy ELBSecurityPolicy-TLS13-1-2-2021-06 \
  --default-actions Type=forward,TargetGroupArn=<tg>
```

- **Set an expiry alarm anyway.** ACM emits `DaysToExpiry` to CloudWatch, and an alarm at 21 days catches a broken validation record before it matters
