### Metrics from logs

```logql
quantile_over_time(0.95,
  {service="orders-api"} | json | unwrap durationMs [5m]
) by (route)
```

- **`unwrap` turns a numeric log field into a metric series**, so p95 latency is available without instrumenting anything
- It is more expensive than a real histogram. **Use it to explore, then add a proper metric for anything you watch continuously**

### The two rules

- **Always narrow the time range first.** Loki scans compressed chunks, and the range is the cost
- **The stream selector must be as specific as you can make it.** `{env="prod"}` alone scans everything
