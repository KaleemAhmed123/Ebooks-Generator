## Service level objectives

- "Is the service healthy" has no answer without a number. **An SLO is that number, agreed in advance**

| Term | Is |
|---|---|
| **SLI** | the measurement. "Percentage of requests under 300ms" |
| **SLO** | the target. "99.5% of requests under 300ms, over 30 days" |
| **error budget** | what the target allows you to fail. 0.5% of requests |
| SLA | a contract with money attached. Most services do not need one |

### What 99.9 percent actually costs

| Availability | Downtime per month |
|---|---|
| 99% | 7 hours 18 minutes |
| 99.9% | **43 minutes** |
| 99.95% | 21 minutes |
| 99.99% | 4 minutes |

- **99.9 percent is a realistic target for a well-run single service.** 99.99 requires multi-zone redundancy and an on-call rotation, and it is a different budget
- **Do not promise more than the dependencies you rely on.** A service on one VPS cannot exceed the provider's own availability
