### Measuring it

```promql
# availability SLI over 30 days
sum(rate(http_requests_total{status!~"5.."}[30d]))
/ sum(rate(http_requests_total[30d]))

# latency SLI: fraction of requests under 300ms
sum(rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket{le="0.3"}[30d]))
/ sum(rate(http_request_duration_seconds_count[30d]))

# error budget remaining, as a fraction
1 - ((1 - <availability>) / 0.001)
```

### What the budget is for

- **Budget remaining: ship.** Deploy often, take risks, run experiments
- **Budget spent: stop shipping features and fix reliability.** That is the whole mechanism, and it converts an argument into a rule
- **Alert on the burn rate, not on the budget being gone.** Burning a month of budget in an hour is a page; burning it evenly over a month is a conversation

- **Do not exclude planned maintenance from the number.** Users experience downtime the same either way
