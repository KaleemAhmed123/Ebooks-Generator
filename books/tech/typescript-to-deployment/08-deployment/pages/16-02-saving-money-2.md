### Spot

- **Up to 90 percent cheaper, and reclaimed with two minutes of warning**
- Right for queue workers, batch jobs, CI runners and anything stateless that can be interrupted
- Wrong for the database, and wrong for a service with no capacity to absorb the loss
- **Fargate Spot is the easiest version**, with a mixed capacity provider keeping a portion on demand

### The largest saving most teams have not made

- **Turning off non-production overnight and at weekends.** A staging environment running 168 hours a week to be used for 40 is a two thirds saving for one scheduled task
