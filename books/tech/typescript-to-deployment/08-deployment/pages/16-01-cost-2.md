### Tagging, which makes all of this answerable

```bash
aws ec2 create-tags --resources i-0abc --tags Key=Service,Value=orders Key=Env,Value=prod Key=Owner,Value=platform
```

- **Activate the tags as cost allocation tags** in the billing console, or they do not appear in reports
- **A tagging policy enforced from day one** is what makes "which team spends what" a query rather than a guess
