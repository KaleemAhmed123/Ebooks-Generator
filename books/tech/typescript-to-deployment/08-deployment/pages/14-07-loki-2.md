## Loki - continued

### The one thing that decides whether Loki works

- **Labels, and only labels, are indexed.** Every unique combination of label values creates a separate stream
- **A label with an unbounded value destroys it.** `requestId`, `userId`, `orderId` or a full URL as a label produces millions of streams and Loki falls over

| Good label | Bad label |
|---|---|
| `service`, `env`, `level` | `request_id` |
| `container`, `host` | `user_id` |
| `job` | `path` with ids in it |

- **Everything else stays in the log line and is searched at query time.** That is the whole design, and fighting it is how people conclude Loki is slow
