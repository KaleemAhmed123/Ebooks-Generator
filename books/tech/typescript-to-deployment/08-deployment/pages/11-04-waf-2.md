### Start in count mode

- **Deploy every rule with `Count` first and watch the metrics for a week.** Managed rule sets block legitimate traffic more often than expected
- A file upload endpoint and a rich text editor are the two that get blocked first
- **Enable request sampling and logging**, or a blocked customer is unexplainable

### Shield

- **Shield Standard is automatic and free**, and it handles the ordinary volumetric attacks against ALB, CloudFront and Route 53
- **Shield Advanced** is a monthly subscription that adds a response team and billing protection. It is for organizations that are actually targeted

- **A WAF sits at CloudFront or at the ALB.** At CloudFront it filters closer to the attacker, and that is the better place
