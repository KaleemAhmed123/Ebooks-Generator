## Caddy - continued

| Directive | Nginx equivalent |
|---|---|
| `reverse_proxy` | `proxy_pass` plus every sensible header |
| `encode zstd gzip` | `gzip on` and the types list |
| `flush_interval -1` | **`proxy_buffering off`**, and just as necessary for SSE |
| `@name` matchers | `location` blocks |
| automatic | `ssl_certificate`, certbot, the renewal timer |
