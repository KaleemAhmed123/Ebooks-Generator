## Node 26 and what changes next

- Released May 2026 as Current, enters LTS in October 2026
- Node 24 is the Active LTS, and is the safe choice for production today

### What landed in 26

- **Temporal enabled by default.** The replacement for `Date`, which has been broken since 1995

```js
const now = Temporal.Now.zonedDateTimeISO("Asia/Kolkata")
const later = now.add({ hours: 3 })
```

- Real time zones, real durations, immutable values, no month-is-zero-indexed trap
- **V8 14.6**, from Chromium 146
- **Test runner additions**: `--test-random-order`, `--test-name-tag`, mock timers for `AbortSignal.timeout`, and diagnostics channel output for OpenTelemetry
- **Permission model is stable**, no longer flagged experimental

### The release schedule is changing

- Node 26 is the last release under the old scheme
- From Node 27 in 2027: **one major per year, year-based version numbers, and every release is LTS**
- The even and odd rule goes away, and so does choosing between Current and LTS

### What to do about it

- Pin the LTS in `engines` and `.nvmrc`
- Upgrading a major becomes a yearly job rather than a twice-yearly decision
