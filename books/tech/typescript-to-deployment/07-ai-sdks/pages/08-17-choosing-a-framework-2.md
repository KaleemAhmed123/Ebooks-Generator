### How to actually decide

- **Start with no framework.** The loop on the first page of this module is twenty lines and it teaches you what you actually need
- **Adopt one when you hit a specific wall**: provider portability, durable pausing, or an integration you do not want to write
- **The choice you cannot easily undo is the state model**, not the library. Keep the conversation in your own tables and any of these becomes replaceable
