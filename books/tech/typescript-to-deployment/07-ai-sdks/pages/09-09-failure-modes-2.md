### Degrading well

- **Decide what the feature does with no model at all.** A support bot falls back to search results and a contact link, and that is a working product
- A feature with no degraded mode should not be on the critical path of anything that matters

### The one that has no error code

- **Quality drift.** A provider updates a model, or your corpus grows, and answers get worse with every metric still green
- The evaluation set from the last two pages is the only detector. Run it on a schedule, not only on a change
