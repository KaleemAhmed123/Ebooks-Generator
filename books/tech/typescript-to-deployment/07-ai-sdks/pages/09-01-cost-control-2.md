## Controlling the spend - continued

- Output tokens are weighted because they cost several times more, so a single counter tracks something close to real money

### The three habits

- **Attribute every call** to a tenant, a user and a feature. Without that, an unexpected bill has no explanation
- **Alert on the derivative, not the total.** Today at four times yesterday is the signal; the monthly total arrives too late
- **Route by difficulty.** A small model on the easy 80 percent of traffic is the largest saving available, and it is a routing change rather than a rewrite
