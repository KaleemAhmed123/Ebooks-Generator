## What it is good at, and what it is not

- Knowing this list is what stops you fighting a tool for an hour on something it was never going to do

### Genuinely good at

| Task | Why |
|---|---|
| **explaining unfamiliar code** | it has read more code than anyone |
| **mechanical refactors across files** | tedious, well-specified, verifiable |
| **boilerplate**: types, DTOs, fixtures, config | high volume, low judgement |
| **tests for existing behavior** | the behavior is right there to read |
| **first drafts of anything** | the blank page is the expensive part |
| **a language or framework you do not know** | it knows the idioms you would look up |
| **regular expressions, SQL, shell** | write-only languages, easy to verify |
