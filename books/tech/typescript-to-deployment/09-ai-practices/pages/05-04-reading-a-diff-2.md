### Ask it to review itself, then verify

```text
Review this diff against the spec. List anything that was changed but not
requested, anything unhandled, and anything you are unsure about.
```

- **A useful first pass and not a substitute.** It finds real things, and it will not report the mistake it was confident about
- **A second model reviewing the first is measurably better than one reviewing itself**, and neither replaces a person on the risky parts
