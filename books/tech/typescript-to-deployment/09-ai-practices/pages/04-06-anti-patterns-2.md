### The long conversation

- Twenty turns of corrections on one task. The context is now mostly failed attempts, and it will repeat them
- **Revert, and start again with everything you learned in one prompt**

### Asking it to decide

```text
Bad:  Should we use Postgres or MongoDB for this?
```

- It will produce a balanced summary and then agree with whatever you lean toward. **It has no stake in the outcome and no context about your team**
- Ask it to argue **against** your choice instead. That produces something useful
