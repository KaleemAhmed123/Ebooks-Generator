### Stop and ask, rather than proceed

```text
If you are unsure about any of this, ask before writing code.
Do not guess at anything I have not specified.
```

- **It defaults to proceeding.** Being asked a question costs a minute; reviewing a wrong assumption costs an hour

### Version control is the real boundary

- **Commit before you start.** A clean tree means `git diff` shows exactly what the agent did, and `git checkout .` undoes all of it
