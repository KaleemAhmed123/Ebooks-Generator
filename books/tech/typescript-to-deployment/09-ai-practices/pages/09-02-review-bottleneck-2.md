### The triage tiers

| Tier | Contains | Review |
|---|---|---|
| **high** | auth, money, migrations, permissions, public API | two humans, line by line |
| **normal** | features, fixes | one human, the Module 5 checklist |
| **low** | tests, docs, dependency bumps, formatting | one human, and lean on CI |

- **Codify the tiers in CODEOWNERS**, so the routing is automatic rather than remembered
