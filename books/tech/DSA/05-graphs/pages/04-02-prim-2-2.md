| Problem | Why it belongs here |
|---|---|
| [Min Cost to Connect All Points](https://leetcode.com/problems/min-cost-to-connect-all-points/) (LeetCode 1584) | Grow MST from any point, greedily pick cheapest cross-edge |
| [Minimum Cost to Reach City With Discounts](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-cost-to-reach-city-with-discounts/) (LeetCode 2093) | Prim-style expansion with a priority queue on edge weights |
| [Connecting Cities With Minimum Cost](https://leetcode.com/problems/connecting-cities-with-minimum-cost/) (LeetCode 1135) | Classic MST on city connections |

- **Time Complexity:** O(E log V), identical to Dijkstra.
- **When to use Prim vs Kruskal:** 
  - If the graph is **Dense** (many edges, E ≈ V²), Prim is faster (especially if implemented with a Fibonacci heap).
  - If the graph is **Sparse** (few edges), Kruskal is easier to write and very fast.
  - In an interview, Kruskal is usually preferred because Union-Find is highly versatile.
