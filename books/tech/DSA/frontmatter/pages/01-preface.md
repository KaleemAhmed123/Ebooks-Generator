## Preface

When I started preparing for coding interviews, I memorized templates — Sliding Window, Two Pointers, Dijkstra's. Then I walked into an interview, saw a problem that did not match any template, and failed.

The industry treats algorithms as a trivia contest: grind hundreds of problems until you recognise them on sight. That approach is brittle. When constraints shift, memorisation shatters.

This book teaches the *derivation* of algorithms, not their final form. We start with brute force, ask why it is slow, find the repeated work, and pick a structure to eliminate it. The optimal algorithm writes itself.

### Who This Book Is For

Engineers tired of guessing. Candidates aiming for top-tier companies who want to build solutions from first principles. Module 08 is for those who want to push into Competitive Programming.
