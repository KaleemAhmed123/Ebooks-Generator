## Recognition drills after Chapter 15 <span class="lv lv1"></span>

Say what sits on top, and when it leaves, before naming a structure. Answers overleaf.

1. Piles of stones: exactly k times, remove half (rounded down) of some pile, the same pile allowed again. Minimise the stones left.
2. Accept a booking `[start, end)` only if it overlaps no booking already accepted; answer each request as it arrives.
3. Find the longest contiguous stretch in which the largest and smallest values differ by at most `limit`.
4. Return the k values that occur most often. The follow-up demands better than O(n log n).
5. Walk past buildings of varying height with some bricks and some ladders. A ladder covers any climb; bricks pay the height difference. How far can you get?
6. For every window of k consecutive numbers, report its median.
7. Two sorted arrays: return the k pairs, one element from each, with the smallest sums.
8. Rearrange a string's letters so no two neighbours are equal, or report that it cannot be done.
9. Count the stretches of exactly k consecutive values whose average is at least a threshold.
10. Given letter frequencies, assign bit codes, none a prefix of another, so the frequency-weighted total length is minimal.
11. Scores arrive one at a time; after each, report the k-th highest so far.
12. Choose at most k engineers. A team scores the sum of their speeds times the lowest efficiency among them. Maximise the score.
