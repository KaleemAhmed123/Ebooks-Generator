## Recognition drills after Chapter 18 <span class="lv lv2"></span>

Trees, heaps, graphs and DP, mixed. For each, answer one of 18-01's four questions (frontier, dominated, boundary, precompute) before any technique. Answers overleaf.

1. Bags of balls; up to `maxOps` times, split one bag into two non-empty bags. Minimise the largest bag.
2. Count the paths in a tree that run downward, start and end anywhere, and sum to a target.
3. Each undirected edge succeeds with its own probability. Find the most reliable route from start to end.
4. Characters have attack and defence. Count those for which some other character is strictly higher in both.
5. Many queries on one string: can `s[l..r]`, rearranged and with up to k letters replaced, become a palindrome?
6. Cut an array into k contiguous non-empty parts so the largest part sum is as small as possible.
7. A 2-D grid of heights: how much rain water stays trapped after it rains?
8. Find the widest pair `i < j` with `a[i] ≤ a[j]`.
9. Weighted roads join up to 100 cities. Find the city with the fewest others within a distance threshold, ties going to the larger number.
10. A grid of empty cells and walls; you may break through at most k walls. Find the fewest steps from corner to corner.
11. Place m balls at given positions on a line so the smallest gap between two balls is as large as possible.
12. Points sorted by x: maximise `yᵢ + yⱼ + |xᵢ − xⱼ|` over pairs with `|xᵢ − xⱼ| ≤ k`.
