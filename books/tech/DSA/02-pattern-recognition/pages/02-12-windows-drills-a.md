## Recognition drills after Chapter 2 <span class="lv lv1"></span>

Name the page before you turn over. No earlier chapter holds a pattern, so one row points ahead instead: a subarray question that is not a window.

1. A shop owner can stay calm for m consecutive minutes, once a day; maximise the customers who leave satisfied.
2. You may replace at most k letters; return the longest run made of one repeated letter.
3. Count the subarrays in which the array's largest value occurs at least k times.
4. Decide whether a non-negative integer c is the sum of two perfect squares.
5. Count the runs of a 0/1 array that hold exactly goal ones.
6. Change up to three values to anything you like; minimise the largest minus the smallest.
7. Erase one run of consecutive values in which no value repeats; maximise the sum erased.
8. Count the triples of stick lengths that can form a triangle.
9. Decide whether some run of at least two values sums to a multiple of k.
10. Take exactly k cards, each from the left or the right end of a row; maximise their total.
11. Move every even number in front of every odd number, in place; any order within a group.
12. Vertical lines stand at each index; choose two that, with the x-axis, hold the most water.
