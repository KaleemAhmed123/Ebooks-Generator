## Recognition drills after Chapter 17 <span class="lv lv2"></span>

Write the signature before the transition; n usually picks it (17-02). Answers overleaf.

1. At most 14 tasks, each a few hours; a work session lasts at most 15 hours and no task may straddle two sessions. Minimise the sessions.
2. Find the longest contiguous stretch whose product is positive.
3. Houses 0 to n − 1 on a line; each of up to 10⁵ buyers offers gold for one contiguous block. Sell each house at most once. Maximise the gold.
4. Players alternately remove the leftmost or rightmost stone and score the sum of the stones still in the row. The first maximises the score difference, the second minimises it. Return the difference.
5. Count the ways to delete characters of `s` so that what remains equals `t`.
6. Up to 40 leaf values in fixed left-to-right order. Every inner node has two children and is worth the largest leaf on its left times the largest leaf on its right. Minimise the sum of inner nodes.
7. Daily prices; trade as often as you like, one share at a time, paying a fixed fee per completed trade.
8. Taking a value earns it, but destroys every copy of the value one above and one below. Maximise the earnings.
9. One move takes 1 from `a[i]` and adds it to `a[i − 1]`; make any number of moves. Minimise the largest value.
10. Pick a subsequence; its score is the sum at its even positions minus the sum at its odd positions. Maximise the score.
11. Two players take turns choosing unused integers from 1 to m, m ≤ 20, adding each to a shared total. Whoever reaches the target first wins. Can the first player force a win?
12. Remove items one at a time; each removal earns the item times its two current neighbours, with 1 beyond the ends. Maximise the total.
