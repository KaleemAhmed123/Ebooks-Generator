## Recognition drills after Chapter 10 <span class="lv lv1"></span>

Name the page before you turn over. Two come from earlier chapters.

1. Score every contiguous subarray as its smallest value times its sum; return the best score.
2. Log lines say a function started or ended at a timestamp, calls nest inside calls; report each function's time excluding the calls it made.
3. Return the index of any value larger than both its neighbours, in O(log n) time.
4. Report the largest reading in every run of k consecutive readings.
5. Each `*` deletes itself and the closest remaining non-star character to its left; return what is left.
6. Prices arrive one per day; for each, report how many consecutive days ending today had a price at most today's.
7. Count the contiguous subarrays that contain exactly k odd numbers.
8. Design a stack whose push, pop, top and current minimum all run in O(1).
9. A string of `(`, `)` and `*`, where `*` may be either bracket or nothing: can it be balanced?
10. Delete exactly k digits from a number string so the rest is as small as possible.
11. Choose a non-empty subsequence with the largest sum, where consecutive chosen indices are at most k apart.
12. Rocks move left or right at one speed; on contact the smaller breaks and equal ones both break; report the survivors.
13. Add up the minimum of every contiguous subarray, modulo 10⁹ + 7.
