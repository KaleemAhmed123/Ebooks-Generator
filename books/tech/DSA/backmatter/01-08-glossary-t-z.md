## Glossary (T–Z)

- **Tabulation:** The Bottom-Up DP approach of iteratively filling a table from base cases up to the final target problem.
- **TLE:** Time Limit Exceeded: the judge stopped the program for running too long.
- **Topological Sort:** A linear ordering of vertices in a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) where for every directed edge U to V, U appears before V.
- **Trie (Prefix Tree):** A tree structure where paths represent strings, allowing O(L) prefix searches and memory compression for shared prefixes.
- **Two Pointers:** Two indices moved by rule: inward on sorted data, reader/writer, or fast/slow, often turning O(N²) pair searches into O(N).
- **Two's Complement:** The signed-integer encoding where −x = ~x + 1.
