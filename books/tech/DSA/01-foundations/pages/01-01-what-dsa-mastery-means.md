## What DSA mastery means

- Mastery is not solving 500 problems. It is recognising structure in a problem you have never seen
- A strong candidate reads the constraints, identifies the structural family, and derives the algorithm. A weak one scrolls through remembered solutions looking for a match
- This book teaches five layers of skill, in order:

- Most interview preparation stops at layer 1 — recognise the pattern, apply the template
- This book pushes toward layer 5 — derive the solution from the structure of the problem
- Every worked example follows a derivation path: brute force → identify the bottleneck → insight → optimised algorithm

### What this book does not teach

- How to write a for loop, what a variable is, or what an array looks like in memory
- The reader already knows basic programming. We start from "I can code, now what?"
