## Glossary: A – B

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **Autoregressive decoding** | generating a sequence one token at a time, feeding each output back in to produce the next | B3 |
| **AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization)** | a 4-bit method that protects the small fraction of weights activations mark as important; fast on GPU serving | B4 · B6 |
| **backpressure** | Slowing intake when downstream is saturated, to keep a system from overloading | B5 · B6 |
| **Backpropagation** | reverse-mode autodiff applied to a neural network's loss | B1 · B2 |
| **Bagging** | training many models on random data subsets and averaging them to cut variance | B1 |
| **Bag of words (BoW)** | representing a document as a vector of word counts, ignoring order | B3 |
| **Barge-in** | letting a user interrupt an assistant by cutting its playback the instant they speak | B2 · B5 · B6 |
| **BART** | an encoder–decoder transformer pretrained by corrupting text and learning to reconstruct it; strong at summarization | B3 |
| **Base model** | the raw output of pretraining: a next-token predictor that continues text but does not yet follow instructions | B4 |
| **Base rate fallacy** | ignoring how rare an event is when reading a test result | B1 |
| **Batch** | a small random sample of data used for one training step | B1 |
| **batch API** | A provider tier for non-interactive work: submit a large job, accept slower turnaround, pay roughly half the interactive rate | B6 |
