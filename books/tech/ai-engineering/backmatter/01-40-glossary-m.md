## Glossary: M

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **MLOps** | the engineering discipline of shipping and maintaining models | B1 |
| **MLP (multi-layer perceptron)** | a network of stacked fully-connected layers | B2 |
| **MLP projector** | The small two-layer network that maps vision-encoder tokens into an LLM's embedding space in LLaVA | B5 |
| **MMDiT (multimodal diffusion transformer)** | a DiT processing image and text tokens together | B2 |
| **MMLU-Pro** | a hard, reasoning-focused multiple-choice benchmark across many subjects | B3 |
| **MMMU** | College-level multimodal reasoning benchmark spanning many subjects with diagrams and formulas | B5 |
| **Model** | the learned rules of an ML system, stored as numbers | B1 |
| **model card** | A structured document disclosing a model's intended use, training data summary, evaluations, limitations, and biases | B6 |
| **model collapse** | Quality degradation when a model is trained on its own unfiltered output | B5 |
| **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** | Open standard connecting AI apps to tools/data via host, client, and server roles, turning M×N integrations into M+N | B5 |
| **Model routing** | sending each request to the cheapest model able to handle it, escalating hard ones to a larger model | B4 |
| **model welfare** | The research question of whether sufficiently sophisticated models could have morally relevant experiences, and what precautions follow under uncertainty | B6 |
