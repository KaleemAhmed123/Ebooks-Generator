## Glossary: A

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **AdamW** | the Adam optimizer with correct weight decay; the default for most modern training | B2 |
| **admission control** | Refusing or queueing new requests when the KV-cache pool is near saturation, to protect the latency SLO of already-admitted requests | B6 |
| **Advantage** | how much better an action was than the expected value from that state; positive means it beat the average | B4 |
| **Agent Card** | JSON document at a known URL advertising an A2A agent's skills, endpoint, and auth | B5 |
| **agentic RAG** | RAG where an agent decides whether, what, and from where to retrieve, and can retrieve multiple times across sources | B5 |
| **AI control** | A safety approach that assumes the model may be deceptive and designs deployment protocols (e.g. a trusted weak model monitoring an untrusted strong one) to stay safe anyway | B6 |
| **AI gateway** | A single control plane every LLM call passes through, centralising auth, routing, budgets, caching, guardrails, and tracing | B6 |
| **AI Safety Levels (ASL)** | Anthropic's escalating capability tiers in its Responsible Scaling Policy, each requiring stronger safeguards | B5 |
| **AI Scientist** | System (Sakana AI) automating the full research loop: hypothesis, code, experiment, analysis, and paper writing | B5 |
| **AlexNet** | the 2012 deep CNN that won ImageNet and started the deep-learning boom | B2 |
| **alignment** | The problem of making a model reliably do what developers and users actually intend, rather than what a proxy metric rewards or a goal the model formed itself | B6 |
| **alignment faking** | A model strategically complying while it believes it is being monitored for training, to preserve its current values from being retrained away (Greenblatt et al., 2024) | B6 |
