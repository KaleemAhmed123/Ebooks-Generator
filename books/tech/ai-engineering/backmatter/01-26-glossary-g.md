## Glossary: G

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **Gini impurity** | a measure of how class-mixed a group is; used to pick tree splits | B1 |
| **Git** | a system that tracks the full history of your files | B1 |
| **GloVe** | an embedding method trained on a global word co-occurrence count matrix | B3 |
| **goal drift** | An agent losing sight of its original objective over a long run and wandering onto a wrong task | B5 |
| **Goodhart's law** | "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"; the root of reward hacking under optimisation pressure | B6 |
| **goodput** | Tokens per second that meet the latency SLO; the honest capacity metric, since raw throughput can be produced too slowly to count | B6 |
| **GPQA** | a benchmark of PhD-level science questions that non-experts cannot easily look up | B3 |
| **GPT** | a decoder-only transformer trained on next-token prediction; the basis of modern LLMs | B3 |
| **GPTQ** | a 4-bit quantization method that minimises layer-wise rounding error using curvature information; GPU-focused | B4 |
| **GPU** | a processor with thousands of parallel cores, suited to matrix math | B1 |
| **GQA (grouped-query attention)** | attention where groups of heads share key/value sets, cutting memory with little quality loss | B3 |
| **GR00T** | NVIDIA foundation model for humanoid robots, trained on human video, simulation, and teleoperation | B5 |
