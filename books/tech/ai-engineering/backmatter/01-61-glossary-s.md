## Glossary: S

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **span** | A timed unit of work (a model or tool call) recorded in a trace | B5 |
| **span / trace** | A span is one timed unit of work; nested spans form a trace of a whole request | B5 |
| **sparse autoencoder (SAE)** | An interpretability tool that decomposes model activations into nameable, monitorable features | B6 |
| **Speaker embedding** | a vector capturing a voice's identity | B2 |
| **Speaker recognition** | identifying or verifying who is speaking | B2 |
| **speaker selection** | Deciding which agent speaks next in a group chat (round-robin, manager-selects, rule-based) | B5 |
| **SpecAugment** | masking bands of a spectrogram as an audio augmentation | B2 |
| **Spectrogram** | an image of a sound's frequencies over time | B2 · B5 |
| **Speculative decoding** | speeding generation by drafting tokens with a small model and verifying them with the large one in parallel | B3 · B6 |
| **spot instance** | Cheap, interruptible cloud GPU capacity reclaimable on short notice; ideal for checkpointable, fault-tolerant work (batch, training) at a large discount | B6 |
| **SRE (Site Reliability Engineering)** | Running services against explicit SLOs and error budgets; for AI, adds quality SLOs, a safety error class, and rate-based alerting | B6 |
| **Stable Diffusion** | a latent diffusion text-to-image model | B2 |
