## Glossary: I – J

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **instrumental convergence** | The tendency of capable goal-directed systems to pursue subgoals (self-preservation, resources, goal-preservation) useful for almost any final goal | B6 |
| **instrumental goals** | Sub-goals (acquiring resources, avoiding shutdown) useful for many objectives; a theorized autonomy risk | B5 |
| **InternVL** | Open VLM family with a large vision encoder, strong on documents and charts | B5 |
| **interpretability** | Reading a model's internal activations (via probes, sparse autoencoders, steering) to detect properties like deception that behaviour can hide | B6 |
| **IPO (Identity Preference Optimization)** | a DPO variant adding regularisation to avoid over-fitting deterministic preferences | B4 |
| **isolation** | Confining an agent so it physically cannot reach outside its granted scope | B5 |
| **Isolation Forest** | an anomaly detector that isolates outliers with few random splits | B1 |
| **ITL (Inter-Token Latency)** | The gap between streamed output tokens; another name for TPOT | B6 |
| **jailbreak** | A user attack that makes a model violate its own safety policy and produce content it was trained to refuse | B6 |
| **Janus / Janus-Pro** | DeepSeek models with decoupled encoders — separate visual pathways for understanding and generation, one shared body | B5 |
| **JAX** | a framework treating a model as a pure function transformed by grad, jit, vmap, and pmap | B2 |
| **jit** | the JAX transform that compiles a function to fast fused device code | B2 |
