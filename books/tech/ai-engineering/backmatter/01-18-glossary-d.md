## Glossary: D

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **DocVQA** | Benchmark for question-answering over document images | B5 |
| **domain randomization** | Randomizing textures, lighting, and physics in simulation so a policy transfers to the real world (sim2real) | B5 |
| **DOM perception** | Perceiving a web page via its HTML structure (precise but brittle) versus by screenshot (vision) | B5 |
| **Dot product** | entry-by-entry multiply-and-sum of two vectors; measures alignment | B1 |
| **DPO (direct preference optimization)** | aligning a model on preference pairs with one supervised-style loss, skipping the reward model and RL loop | B4 |
| **DQN (deep Q-network)** | a neural network estimating Q-values, stabilised by a replay buffer and a target network; learns discrete-action tasks from raw input | B4 |
| **Draft model** | a small fast model that proposes tokens for a larger model to verify in speculative decoding | B3 |
| **Dropout** | randomly switching off neurons during training to reduce overfitting | B2 |
| **DSPy** | Framework where you declare typed signatures and a compiler generates and optimises the prompts against a metric | B5 |
| **dual-use** | Capability that helps both legitimate and malicious users; the safety concern is marginal uplift to a malicious actor | B6 |
| **durable execution** | Making a long workflow survive crashes: persisted state, resume from last step, idempotent side effects | B5 |
| **Dying ReLU** | a neuron stuck outputting zero with no gradient, unable to recover | B2 |
