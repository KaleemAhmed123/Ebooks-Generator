## Glossary: T

| Term | Means | In |
|---|---|---|
| **Tensor** | the generalization of vectors and matrices to any number of dimensions | B1 · B2 |
| **tensor parallelism (TP)** | Splitting each layer's matrices across GPUs to fit a model too large for one card; interconnect-bound, rarely scales past one node | B6 |
| **TensorRT-LLM** | NVIDIA's inference library that compiles a model to a hardware-specific engine for peak performance, at the cost of a build step and NVIDIA lock-in | B6 |
| **Term frequency (TF)** | how often a word appears in a single document | B3 |
| **Test set** | held-out data touched once, for the honest final score | B1 |
| **Text-to-speech (TTS)** | generating speech audio from text | B2 |
| **TF-IDF** | term frequency weighted by inverse document frequency, downweighting common words | B3 |
| **theory of mind (ToM)** | Modelling what other agents know, want, and will do; needed for good coordination | B5 |
| **thinker-talker** | Omni-model split: a thinker reasons and produces text while a lightweight talker streams speech in parallel | B5 |
| **thread (LangGraph)** | One conversation's persisted state, keyed by thread_id, giving short-term memory | B5 |
| **Throughput** | how fast a processor computes | B1 |
| **Time series** | data with a time order, where the past predicts the future | B1 |
