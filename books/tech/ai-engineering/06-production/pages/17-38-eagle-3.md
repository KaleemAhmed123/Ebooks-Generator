## EAGLE-3

- The quality of speculative decoding lives entirely in the draft: a better draft is accepted more often, and acceptance rate is the whole game. **EAGLE-3** is the current state-of-the-art drafting method as of 2026. **[VERIFY version/results]**
- Its idea: instead of a separate small model guessing tokens, draft at the **feature level** — reuse the target model's own internal hidden states (features) to predict the next tokens, and train the draft head on multiple layers of those features. A draft that thinks in the target's representation matches it far more often.

- **The lineage:** Medusa added extra prediction heads; EAGLE draft at the feature level; EAGLE-2 made the draft tree dynamic; EAGLE-3 trains on multiple feature layers and removes a constraint that had capped how much training data helped — pushing accepted-tokens-per-pass higher again. **[VERIFY lineage details]**
- You do not usually implement it — you enable it. It ships as a drafting option in the serving engines, and tools like SpecForge train EAGLE-3 draft heads for a given target model.

:::interview
**"How would you cut TTFT/TPOT without changing the model's outputs?"** Speculative decoding — it is lossless, so quality is untouched. I would enable a strong drafter (EAGLE-3 as of 2026) in the serving engine, measure the **acceptance rate** on my real traffic (that, not the paper's number, sets the speedup), and confirm the win holds at my batch size — speculation helps most with spare compute and can regress at max saturation. Lossless, measured on real traffic, sensitive to load: those three points are the signal.
:::
