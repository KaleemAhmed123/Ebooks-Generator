## "Tell me about an AI project that failed, or you'd do differently."

- **What they're screening for:** self-awareness and learning — a candidate who's never failed either hasn't shipped much or won't own it.
- **A strong answer shows:**
  - **A real failure with honest ownership** — your part in it, not "the data team's fault."
  - **The root cause** — often instructive: no eval set so quality was unmeasured, a demo that didn't survive production, over-engineering before validating the need, solving a problem users didn't have, or ignoring cost until it exploded.
  - **The specific lesson** — concrete and transferable ("now I build an eval set before writing the feature"), not a platitude.
  - **Evidence you applied it** — you did it differently next time with a better outcome.
- Pick a failure with a clear lesson that shows growth, and keep the blame on your decisions.

:::warn
Weak: "My only weakness is I work too hard" / blaming others. Strong: a genuine failure, honest ownership, a specific root cause, and the concrete change you made afterwards.
:::

:::interview
What's really being tested: self-awareness, ownership, and the ability to extract a transferable lesson from failure and prove you applied it.
:::
