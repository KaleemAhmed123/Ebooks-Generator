## "Everything is tagged 'add AI.' How do you prioritize?"

- **What they're screening for:** product prioritisation under hype pressure — picking what's valuable *and* feasible, not loudest.
- **A strong answer shows:**
  - **Score on value × feasibility** — expected user/business impact against technical feasibility (can the model do it reliably?), effort, and cost/risk.
  - **Prefer high-value, high-feasibility, low-risk** first — quick wins that build trust and data; defer moonshots that the tech can't yet support.
  - **Kill the AI-for-AI's-sake items** — where a non-AI solution is better, say so.
  - **Factor reversibility and learning** — ship cheap pilots that teach you something, measure, then double down on winners.
  - **Align with stakeholders** on the criteria so prioritisation is transparent, not a black box.
- Essentially a feasibility-aware impact/effort prioritisation, with a bias toward measurable pilots.
