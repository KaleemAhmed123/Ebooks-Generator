# Large Language Models

## What is RLHF, and why is it needed after pretraining and SFT?

- A pretrained model predicts likely text; **SFT** (supervised fine-tuning on demonstrations) teaches it to follow instructions. But "likely" and "well-written" still aren't "what humans prefer" — helpful, honest, harmless, correctly formatted.
- **RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback)** closes that gap in three steps:
  1. Collect human **preferences**: for a prompt, people rank/compare model outputs.
  2. Train a **reward model** to predict those preferences (a score for any response).
  3. **Optimise the LLM** (via PPO) to maximise that reward, with a KL penalty keeping it near the SFT model.
- Why not just more SFT? Demonstrations teach *one* good answer; preferences teach the model to **rank** across the whole space of its own outputs, including avoiding bad ones — a richer signal.
