## "The field changes weekly. How do you stay current without drowning?"

- **What they're screening for:** a sustainable, signal-over-noise habit — and the judgment to tell durable fundamentals from hype.
- **A strong answer shows:**
  - **A filtered input diet** — a few trusted sources (key labs' papers/blogs, primary docs, a couple of curators) rather than chasing every launch tweet.
  - **Fundamentals-first** — you prioritise concepts that transfer (attention, RAG, evals, serving) over memorising this week's model names, because specifics churn and principles don't.
  - **Learn by doing** — you try new tools/models on a real task or a small eval, not just read about them. Hands-on beats headlines.
  - **Depth on demand** — go deep when a project needs it, stay aware otherwise; you can't deeply learn everything.
- Bonus: a way to decide what's worth your time (does it change how I'd build something?).

:::warn
Weak: "I read Twitter/X all day" (noise, no filter) or "I don't have time to keep up." Strong: a curated source list + fundamentals-first + trying things hands-on, with a filter for what actually matters.
:::

What's really being tested: a sustainable learning system that prioritises transferable fundamentals and hands-on validation over hype-chasing.
