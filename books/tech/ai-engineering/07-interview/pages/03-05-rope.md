## What is RoPE, and why has it become the default position scheme?

- **RoPE (rotary position embedding)** encodes position by **rotating** the query and key vectors by an angle proportional to their position, in 2-D subspace pairs. [VERIFY: still dominant as of 2026.]
- The elegant consequence: when you dot a rotated query with a rotated key, the result depends only on their **relative** offset (position i − j), not absolute positions. Attention naturally becomes relative.
- Why it won:
  - **No extra parameters** and applied inside attention, not added to embeddings.
  - **Relative** by construction — matches how language works.
  - **Extends** to longer contexts via tricks like position interpolation / NTK scaling, enabling the long-context models you see today.
- It's the reason "extend the context window" is often a cheap post-hoc rescale of RoPE frequencies rather than a retrain.
