## "Why AI engineering? What excites you about it?"

- **What they're screening for:** genuine, grounded motivation — not buzzword enthusiasm — and whether you understand what the work actually is.
- **A strong answer shows:**
  - **Specificity** — what concretely draws you (building systems people use, the blend of ML and real engineering, the pace, a problem domain you care about), with a real example from your own work.
  - **Realism** — you're excited *and* clear-eyed: you know the job is as much evals, data, serving, and reliability as it is model magic. That signals you'll stay when the novelty wears off.
  - **Alignment** — your interest matches what this role/company actually does.
  - **A point of view** — you've formed opinions from hands-on experience, not just headlines.
- Avoid generic hype ("AI is the future, it's revolutionary") with no substance — that reads as someone chasing a trend.

:::warn
Weak: "AI is the future and it's super exciting." Strong: a specific thing you've built and loved, plus clear-eyed awareness that the craft is evals/data/serving/reliability, not just prompting a model.
:::

:::interview
What's really being tested: authentic, specific motivation grounded in real experience and a realistic view of the work — that you'll be engaged for the unglamorous 90%, not just the hype.
:::
