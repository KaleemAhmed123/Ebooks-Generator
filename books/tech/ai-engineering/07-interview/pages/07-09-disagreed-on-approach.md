## "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate on an AI approach."

- **What they're screening for:** how you handle technical conflict — with evidence and respect, not ego — which matters in a fast-moving field full of strong opinions.
- **A strong answer shows:**
  - **The substance** — e.g. fine-tune vs RAG, build vs buy, which model — stated fairly from both sides.
  - **How you resolved it with data** — proposed an **eval or a quick experiment** to settle it empirically rather than arguing from authority. "We A/B'd both on a small eval set and let the numbers decide."
  - **Disagree and commit** — once decided (even if against your view), you backed it fully.
  - **The outcome and what you learned** — including times you were wrong, which signals maturity.
- The best answers show opinions held loosely and resolved by evidence — exactly how you'd want AI decisions made.

:::warn
Weak: "I was right and convinced them" (ego) or "I just went along" (no spine). Strong: framed the disagreement, ran a small eval to decide, committed to the result, and noted the learning.
:::

:::interview
What's really being tested: evidence-based conflict resolution (run an experiment, let data decide) plus disagree-and-commit — not who won the argument.
:::
